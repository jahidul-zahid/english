Part 7 — Your main improvement targets

1. Verb tenses and verb forms

Practice the difference between present and past: wake up → woke up, give → gave, and start → started.

2. Sentence structure

Practice question word order: “How can I…?” rather than “How I can…?”

3. Articles and prepositions

Notice phrases such as “a software developer” and “save notes in Google Keep.”

4. Natural phrasing

Learn complete everyday expressions, not just individual words.

5. Speaking smoothly

Finish your thought without restarting too often. Speak a little more slowly and clearly.

Daily practice routine

  1. Listen: Play the Mark audio and follow the text.
  2. Repeat: Pause after each sentence and say it out loud.
  3. Recall: Hide the text and try to say the sentence from memory.
  4. Use: Make one new sentence using the same pattern.
  5. Review: Revisit today's difficult sentences tomorrow.

Aim for steady improvement, not a perfect accent. Clear, natural, confident communication is the goal.
