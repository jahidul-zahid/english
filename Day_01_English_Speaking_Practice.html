Part 7 — Your main improvement targets

Analysis of your Day 01 sentences. Your ideas are clear and your vocabulary is good (opportunities, expand my network, improve). Most mistakes are small changes to verbs, not missing words — so they are fixable. Work on these in order: issues 1 to 3 first.

1. You add -s and -ing to verbs that should stay plain

Your biggest issue — about 10 sentences.

❌ I takes my breakfast  →  ✅ I take / I had breakfast

❌ I wants to remove this  →  ✅ I want to overcome this

❌ This will helps  →  ✅ This will help

❌ Mistakes will not happens  →  ✅ Mistakes will not happen

❌ I am struggling to talking  →  ✅ I'm struggling to talk

Rule: The verb stays in its plain form after I / you / we / they, after will / can, and after to. The -s only appears with he / she / it (she works).

Practice: Write 10 sentences a day using these frames and say each one out loud: I ___ every day. · I will ___. · I want to ___. · I can ___. Then repeat the same sentence with she to feel the difference.

2. You tell past stories in the present tense

About 8 sentences.

❌ I'm wake up late  →  ✅ I woke up late

❌ I start my computer  →  ✅ I started my computer

❌ I enjoy it today, so I came again  →  ✅ I enjoyed it today, so I came back

❌ I work up at 9 o'clock  →  ✅ I woke up at 9 o'clock

Rule: If the time is yesterday, this morning, last week, today (already finished), every verb in the story is past.

Practice: Every night tell your day in 5 sentences starting with “This morning I…”. Keep a short list and check it before speaking: wake → woke · take → took · give → gave · come → came · start → started.

3. You skip or mix up “am / is / are”

About 6 sentences.

❌ I working with Laravel  →  ✅ I work with Laravel

❌ You sure  →  ✅ You're sure

❌ What you facing?  →  ✅ What are you facing?

❌ I'm wake up  →  ✅ I woke up

Rule: am / is / are goes only before an -ing word or an adjective (I'm working, I'm sure). If the verb is not -ing, do not use am.

Practice: Make two columns. Left (happening now): I'm working on… I'm learning… I'm trying to… Right (always true): I work with… I use… I build… Say 3 of each daily about your Laravel work.

4. You translate word for word, so the phrase sounds wrong

About 10 sentences. This looks like Bangla thinking put straight into English.

❌ Share me what you're facing  →  ✅ Tell me what you're facing

❌ Myself, Jahidul Islam  →  ✅ I'm Jahidul Islam

❌ I want to remove this from my end  →  ✅ I want to overcome this

❌ I feel rush  →  ✅ I feel rushed

❌ Keep notes into Google Keep  →  ✅ Save notes in Google Keep

Rule: Single words will not fix this. Learn whole ready-made chunks instead.

Practice: Keep a “phrase bank” in Google Keep with chunks only: tell me about… · help me with… · I'm struggling with… · I'd like to… · Do you think…? Learn 3 new chunks a day and use each in 2 sentences about your real work.

5. Long sentences fall apart

Sentences 28, 33 and 34.

❌ I actually you not don't not have my plugins my Google Drive, I will available that then I will come back  →  ✅ I don't have Google Drive connected yet. Once I connect it, I'll come back.

Rule: You started speaking before you knew how the sentence would end. Use the 2-sentence rule: one idea, one short sentence, then a connector (so, because, but, then, once).

Practice: Say your idea slowly in two short sentences first. When that feels easy, speed up.

6. Questions are built in the wrong order

About 4 sentences.

❌ How I can explain or handle our foreign client?  →  ✅ How can I communicate with foreign clients?

❌ What's your problem actually facing?  →  ✅ What problem are you facing?

Rule: Question word + can / do / are + subject + verb.

Practice: Learn 5 fixed starters and only change the ending: How can I…? · What do you…? · Why are you…? · Where can I…? · Do you think…?

7. Smaller issues: articles

About 3 sentences.

❌ I am software developer  →  ✅ I'm a software developer

❌ I have a idea  →  ✅ I have an idea

Rule: Use a / an before a job title and before a singular thing. an goes before a vowel sound.

Practice: Say the whole phrase “I'm a ___” until it feels automatic.

Daily practice routine

  1. Listen: Play the Mark audio and follow the text.
  2. Repeat: Pause after each sentence and say it out loud.
  3. Recall: Hide the text and try to say the sentence from memory.
  4. Use: Make one new sentence using the same pattern.
  5. Review: Revisit today's difficult sentences tomorrow.

Aim for steady improvement, not a perfect accent. Clear, natural, confident communication is the goal.

Your daily 15-minute speaking plan

  1. 5 min — Past tense: Tell your day in 5 sentences, starting with “This morning I…” (issue 2).
  2. 5 min — Plain verbs: Say 10 sentences using the frames I ___ / I will ___ / I want to ___ (issue 1).
  3. 3 min — Phrase bank: Add 3 chunks to Google Keep and use each one (issue 4).
  4. 2 min — Record: Record one 2-sentence idea and listen back (issue 5).

Focus on issues 1 to 3 for the first two weeks. Add issue 4 after that.
