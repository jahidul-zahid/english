Part 8 — Your main improvement targets

Analysis of your Day 02 sentences. Your meaning is always clear — the problems are small word-order and verb-form slips. Issues 1, 2 and 3 also appeared on Day 01, so they are your core patterns. Practice those first.

1. Question word order is still the same problem

Lessons 1, 2, 3 and 5 — this was also an issue on Day 01, so it is one of your core patterns.

❌ How I measure that my English is improving?  →  ✅ How can I tell if my English is getting better?

❌ How many days it can take?  →  ✅ How long will it take?

❌ How I can give a presentation…?  →  ✅ How can I give a presentation…?

Rule: Question word + can / will / do + subject + verb. The helper word comes before I / it / you.

Practice: Use these 4 starters and change only the ending: How can I…? · How long will it…? · What should I…? · Will this…? Make 5 new questions about your work today and say them out loud.

2. After “want / asked me / to”, the verb must stay plain

Lessons 6, 8, 12 and 14.

❌ I want go to speak like a native  →  ✅ I want to speak like a native speaker

❌ He asked me to told a fixed amount  →  ✅ He asked me to give him a fixed rate

❌ They hire me for improve their feature  →  ✅ They're hiring me to improve their features

Rule: want + to + plain verb, asked me + to + plain verb, to + plain verb. Never to told or to going.

Practice: Fill the gap out loud with a plain verb: I want to ___. · He asked me to ___. · They hired me to ___. 3 sentences about your real clients.

3. You drop the small helper words (am / do / does) and the subject

Lessons 11, 14 and 17.

❌ I actually good at admin panel  →  ✅ I'm good at admin panels

❌ I not find the suitable sentence  →  ✅ I can't find the right sentence

❌ If work need to four to six hours…  →  ✅ If you need me to work four to six hours…

Rule: Every sentence needs a subject and a helper when the verb is not am/is/are: I can't find… / I don't know… For adjectives use I'm good at…

Practice: Say 5 sentences with I'm good at… · I'm not good at… · I can't… · I don't… about your Laravel skills.

4. Tense: past for what happened, present for what is generally true

Lessons 7, 13 and 21.

❌ It took time and practice… it's can be achievable  →  ✅ It takes time and practice… it's achievable

❌ My last job pay me 15,000  →  ✅ My last job paid me 15,000

❌ I gave… They said they will decide, then they reach me  →  ✅ They said they'd decide and get back to me

Rule: General truth → present (it takes time). Finished event → past (paid, gave, said). And it's can is never correct — say it can or it's.

Practice: Retell the client call in 4 past-tense sentences: I offered… · They said… · I told them… · They'll get back to me.

5. Business and negotiation words: you need exact phrases

Lessons 8 to 16. This is new on Day 02 and it is where your meaning gets lost under pressure.

❌ My budget is almost fixed  →  ✅ My rate is almost fixed

❌ I go twenty K  →  ✅ I'd charge 20,000 per month

❌ Not very good deal… but for now it can go  →  ✅ It's not ideal, but I can make it work for now

❌ Work for project basis  →  ✅ It's project-based

Rule: A freelancer talks about a rate, not a budget. Use I'd charge… / my rate would be… / that's the lowest I can go.

Practice: Learn these 5 chunks and say your two-rate offer using all of them: my rate would be · per month · I have a little room to negotiate · that's the lowest I can go · let me know what you decide.

6. Finishing the thought (today's main target)

Lessons 17, 19 and 23.

❌ …I lost this sentence. I not find the suitable sentence how to finish  →  ✅ I know what I want to say, but I struggle to find the right words.

Rule: You do not need a perfect sentence. End with a ready-made closing phrase, then stop.

Practice: Memorize 3 endings and use one in every practice: That's my offer for now. · That's the lowest I can go. · Let me know what you decide.
